Title: Typhoid conjugate vaccines for preventing typhoid fever (enteric fever)
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-06-14

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To assess the efficacy and safety of typhoid conjugate vaccines compared to no vaccine, placebo,
typhoid-inactive agents (vaccines for another disease), or other typhoid vaccines for preventing
morbidity and mortality associated with typhoid fever infection in adults and children.
