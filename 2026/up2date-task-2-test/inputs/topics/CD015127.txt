Title: Safety and efficacy of proton pump inhibitors in preterm infants with gastroesophageal reflux disease

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To assess the safety and efficacy of proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) for the treatment of preterm
infants with diagnosed or suspected gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD).
