Title: Hormonal contraception for women at risk of HIV infection.
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-09-27

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To determine the effects of hormonal contraception on HIV acquisition in women who live in settings
with high HIV prevalence.
