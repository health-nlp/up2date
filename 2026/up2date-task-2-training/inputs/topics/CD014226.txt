Title: Fenestrated endovascular repair for abdominal aortic aneurysms
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-07-06

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To evaluate the benefits and harms of complex stent-graft fenestrated endovascular aneurysm repair
(FEVAR) versus open surgical repair or conservative (non-operative) management for people with
complex abdominal aortic aneurysms.
