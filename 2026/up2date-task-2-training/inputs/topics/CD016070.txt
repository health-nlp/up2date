Title: Parallel use of low-complexity automated nucleic acid amplification tests and lateral flow urine lipoarabinomannan assays to detect tuberculosis disease in adults and adolescents living with HIV
Year: 2024

Date: 2024-05-13

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (diagnostic). The objectives are as follows: To
compare the diagnostic accuracy of parallel use of lateral flow urine lipoarabinomannan on urine and
low-complexity automated nucleic acid amplification tests on respiratory samples versus each test
alone for detection of tuberculosis disease in adults and adolescents living with HIV who present
with presumptive tuberculosis. Secondary objectives To investigate the following sources of
heterogeneity: clinical setting; signs and symptoms of tuberculosis; screening positivity for
tuberculosis disease by chest x-ray, C-reactive protein elevation, and molecular World Health
Organization-recommended rapid diagnostics; seriously ill; advanced HIV; and CD4 cell count.
