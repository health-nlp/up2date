Title: Undernutrition as a risk factor for tuberculosis disease
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-10-06

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (prognosis). The objectives are as follows: To
determine the prognostic value of undernutrition in the general population of adults and children
for predicting tuberculosis disease. Population Adults and children from the general population
Index prognostic factors Undernutrition (wasting, stunting, underweight) Outcomes Incidence of
tuberculosis (adjusted risk) Timing Short- and long-term follow-up Setting General
population/outpatients Study design Cohort studies
