Title: Acupuncture for neonatal abstinence syndrome in newborn infants
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-08-22

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To assess if acupuncture reduces the treatment duration of neonatal abstinence syndrome (NAS) in
newborn infants, reduces adverse events and reduces length of hospital stay.
