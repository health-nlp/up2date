Title: Healthcare workers’ informal uses of mobile devices to support their work: a qualitative evidence synthesis
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-07-21

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (qualitative). The objectives are as follows: To
explore the views, experiences, and practices of healthcare workers, managers and other
professionals working in healthcare services regarding their informal, innovative uses of mobile
devices to support their work.
