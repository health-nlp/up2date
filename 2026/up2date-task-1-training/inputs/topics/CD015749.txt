Title: Multimodal interventions for cachexia management
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-06-27

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To assess: the effects of multimodal interventions which aim to alleviate and/or stabilise cachexia
(disease-related wasting) in people with a chronic illness; and the safety of multimodal
interventions when given to people with cachexia.
