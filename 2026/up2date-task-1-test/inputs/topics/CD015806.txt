Title: TB-LAMP (loop-mediated isothermal amplification) for diagnosing pulmonary tuberculosis in children
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-09-07

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (diagnostic). The objectives are as follows: To
determine the diagnostic accuracy of tuberculosis loop-mediated isothermal amplification (TB-LAMP)
for the detection of pulmonary tuberculosis in children with presumptive pulmonary tuberculosis.
Secondary objectives To compare the diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP and Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra for the
detection of pulmonary tuberculosis in children with presumptive pulmonary tuberculosis. To compare
the diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP and smear microscopy for detecting pulmonary tuberculosis in
children when TB-LAMP is considered as a replacement test for smear microscopy. To determine the
diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP for the detection of pulmonary tuberculosis if used as an add-on test
amongst sputum smear-negative children. To investigate potential sources of heterogeneity in the
diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP due to factors such as smear status, age, gender, HIV status,
setting, and tuberculosis burden.
