Title: Community views on mass drug administration for soil-transmitted helminths: a qualitative evidence synthesis
Year: 2024

Date: 2024-01-05

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (qualitative). The objectives are as follows: To
synthesize qualitative research evidence about community experience with, and perception of, mass
drug administration (MDA) programmes for soil-transmitted helminths (STHs). To assess how findings
confirm, extend, enrich, or conflict with those previously identified for MDA programmes for
lymphatic filariasis.
