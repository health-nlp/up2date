Title: Multifaceted behavioral interventions to improve treatment adherence of glaucoma
Year: 2023

Date: 2023-05-31

Abstract:
Objectives This is a protocol for a Cochrane Review (intervention). The objectives are as follows:
To assess the impact of two or more behavioral interventions (i.e. multifaceted) on topical glaucoma
therapy adherence in individuals utilizing intraocular-lowering therapy for at least three months
prior to intervention for ocular hypertension or glaucoma.
